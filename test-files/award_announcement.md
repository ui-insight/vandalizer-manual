## Meridian Innovation Fund Announces 2026 "Catalyst Grant" Recipient

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**COSTA MESA, CA – November 2, 2025** – The **Meridian Innovation Fund (MIF)** is delighted to announce the selection of **NCURA Regional Workshop (NRW)** as the recipient of the prestigious 2026 Catalyst Grant. This competitive award recognizes pioneering research or capacity-building projects with the potential for high-impact, interdisciplinary breakthroughs in research management.

---

### **Project & Recipient Details**

| Detail | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Recipient Organization** | **NCURA Regional Workshop (NRW) – Region VII** |
| **Principal Investigator (PI)/Project Lead** | Ms. Cassandra "Cassie" Morales (Director of NRW Operations) |
| **Co-Investigator** | Dr. Michael Chen, specializing in academic compliance informatics |
| **Project Title** | Nexus of Regulatory Competency: A Multimodal Approach to Hyper-Dimensional Data Mapping in Ephemeral Systems |
| **MIF Award ID** | MIF-CG-2026-47A-Alpha-**NRW** |
| **Grant Type** | Catalyst Grant (Tier III: Rapid Deployment - **Capacity Building Focus**) |
| **Mandatory Reporting Schedule** | Quarterly financial reports, semi-annual technical progress summaries on module deployment, and a final workshop attendee feedback assessment within 60 days of project completion. |
| **Data Sharing Requirement** | All non-proprietary workshop curricula and anonymized pre/post-training evaluation data must be deposited in the MIF Open Repository (MOR) within nine months of the project's start date. |

* **Projected Completion Date:** January 31, 2029
* **Total Duration:** 36 Months (Three Years)
* **Mid-Term Review Milestone:** August 15, 2027 (Focus on completion of Modules 1-4 development)
